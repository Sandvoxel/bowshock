# BuilderScreen

The full Build-mode layout, shown in **Cutaway**: fin A of a linked three-fin set is selected and its height is mid-drag. Use this as the reference for composition and density.

**Regions**: Parts bin (rail + sized list) on the left · viewport on top with **Station Lanes** below it in the centre · inspector on the right · status strip along the bottom.

What to notice:
- **One colour per system, everywhere**: the magenta motor and mount in the cutaway are the magenta Propulsion bars below, the lavender chute matches Recovery, and the green altimeter and ballast match Avionics & payload. Cut walls, rings and the bulkhead are hatched.
- **The camera is side-on, so the lanes line up with the model**: every part, CG and CP sits directly above its bar or line.
- **Selection**: solid `accent` brackets on the clicked fin (A), dashed on its linked siblings, brackets on the *Fins ×3* bar, the inspector titled *Fin set · A* with `LINKED ×3`, and the Height field in its drag state.
- **View toolbar**: 3D / 2D · **Solid / Cutaway / X-ray / Exploded** · snap step. Symmetry is picked in the Parts bin footer, KSP-style. The round handle at the nose end of the axis rotates the cut plane.
- **Drawing-sheet details**: corner registration marks, scale bar, mono uppercase labels, ruled cells, and flat framed overlays with no shadows.
