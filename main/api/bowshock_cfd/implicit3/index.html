Module implicit3 

Source
Expand description

Line-implicit pseudo-time stepping for Solver3: the three-factor approximate factorization (“ADI”) of crate::implicit with 5x5 blocks.

One iteration solves (I/dt - dR/dU) dU = R with the right-hand side R of the full (second-order, limited) residual and the first-order flux Jacobians on the left, factorised by direction and solved i, then j, then k:

  (I - dt J_i) X = dt R,   (I - dt J_j) Y = X,   (I - dt J_k) dU = Y,   U <- U + dU  (halved until rho, p > 0)

Face linearisation, entropy-fixed Roe |A|, shock-sensor blend, ghost Jacobians and the defect-correction loop (r = b - (A_i du + A_j du + A_k du - 2 du)) are those of implicit.rs. Differences:

  • Lines are cut at solid cells: a solid cell is the identity row (d = I, lo = up = 0, rhs = 0), so a line through a fin solves as independent segments. A fluid cell next to a solid cell along the line drops that off-diagonal block B and adds B T to its diagonal, T the reflection about the wall face normal (as for a wall boundary).
  • Ghost Jacobians on the first / last cell of a line: characteristic far field T = 0 (frozen ghost), slip wall and symmetry planes T = reflection3(n_face), the axis T = 0 (the ghost depends on another cell, frozen); ImplicitParams::implicit_bc = false freezes the wall ones too.
  • Euler only: no source term and no viscous terms.
