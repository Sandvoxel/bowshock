Menter SST k-omega turbulence model (2003 version) for the viscous solver (Turbulence::Sst).

The model transports the specific turbulence kinetic energy k and the specific dissipation rate omega of every cell (Solver::turb, [k, omega]) with a loosely coupled pseudo-time step at the end of every Solver::step; the eddy viscosity mu_t it produces (Solver::mu_t) enters the mean-flow equations through the viscous terms of viscous.rs. Units as in viscous.rs (rho_inf = c_inf = 1, lengths in nose diameters, nu = mu / rho).

  constants: a1 = 0.31, beta* = 0.09;  set 1: sigma_k 0.85, sigma_w 0.5, beta 0.075, gamma 5/9;
             set 2: sigma_k 1.0, sigma_w 0.856, beta 0.0828, gamma 0.44;    phi = F1 phi_1 + (1 - F1) phi_2
  free stream: k_inf = 9e-9,  omega_inf = 1e-6 re_c                         (mu_t / mu = 0.009)
  d = distance of the cell centroid to the nearest no-slip wall face
  S^2 = 2 (u_x^2 + v_r^2 + (v/r)^2) + (u_r + v_x)^2 - 2/3 div^2,   S = sqrt(max(S^2, 0))     (cell gradients)
  X   = 2 rho sigma_w2 (grad k . grad omega) / omega,                CD = max(X, 1e-20)
  F1  = tanh(a^4),  a = min( max( sqrt(k) / (beta* omega d), 500 nu / (d^2 omega) ), 4 rho sigma_w2 k / (CD d^2) )
  F2  = tanh(b^2),  b = max( 2 sqrt(k) / (beta* omega d), 500 nu / (d^2 omega) )
  mu_t = min( rho a1 k / max(a1 omega, S F2), 1e5 / re_c )
  P    = min( mu_t S^2, 10 beta* rho k omega )
  rho dk/dt     = (-conv_k + diff_k) / V + P - beta* rho omega k
  rho domega/dt = (-conv_w + diff_w) / V + gamma rho P / max(mu_t, 1e-30) - beta rho omega^2 + (1 - F1) X
  conv = sum over the four faces of (outward mass flow) (phi_upwind - phi_c), inflow faces only; the mass flow of a face is
         `F[0] w_f` of the stored inviscid face flux
  diff = sum over the four faces of the outward (mu_f + sigma_f mu_t,f) (g_f . n) w_f, `g_f` the corrected face gradient of
         `viscous.rs` (a compact normal derivative with the averaged tangential part), `sigma_f` the mean of the blended
         `sigma` of the two sides

Boundary states of the two scalars (the ghost of a face, mirrored in the face centre like the mean-flow ghosts): Bc::Freestream | Characteristic | PressureOutflow take (k_inf, omega_inf); Extrapolate, slip walls and the axis the cell values; a no-slip wall k_ghost = -k_c, omega_ghost = 2 omega_w - omega_c, omega_w = 60 nu_c / (0.075 d_c^2) (the face values are then k = 0, omega = omega_w). With a wall roughness ks > 0 (Viscous::roughness, equivalent sand grain in nose diameters) the wall cells take the Aupoix (2015) “Colebrook” rough-wall values of aupoix_wall: k_ghost = 2 k_w - k_c, omega_ghost = 2 omega_w - omega_c, and the face eddy viscosity of a no-slip face is the wall value mu_t,w = rho k_w / omega_w instead of 0 (Solver::rough_wall, read by viscous::face_mu: the mean-flow wall shear is (mu + mu_t,w) u_c / d_c). The rough state is for the body surface (Walls::rough_jface; the base wall and the sting keep the smooth state) and its roughness is phased in over the first ROUGH_RAMP_CELLS wall cells (rough_ramp); ks = 0 takes none of these paths.

The update is a sequence of data-parallel kernels (update): cell gradients of k, omega; the blending functions; the face coefficients; the per-cell residual and the coefficients of the two linear systems; the line solves; the clipped update with the new eddy viscosity. Per equation, with D_k = beta* omega, D_w = 2 beta omega, M- / M+ the mass flow in the positive direction through the lower / upper face of a line direction and G = (mu_f + sigma_f mu_t,f) w_f / |d|:

  diag0 = rho V / dt + rho V D
  direction dir:  lo = -(max(M-, 0) + G-),  up = -(max(-M+, 0) + G+),  dd = max(M-, 0) + max(-M+, 0) + G- + G+
  i lines:  (diag0 + dd_i) x_c + lo_i x_{c-1} + up_i x_{c+1} = V R_c                    R_c = rho dphi/dt
  j lines:  (1 + dd_j / diag0) dphi_c + (lo_j / diag0) dphi_{c-1} + (up_j / diag0) dphi_{c+1} = x_c
  phi_new = max(phi + dphi, 0.1 phi), then k >= 1e-3 k_inf, omega >= 1e-3 omega_inf;  then mu_t is recomputed

A neighbour that is a ghost or a solid cell drops its off-diagonal (the diagonal part stays). The two scalars are solved together with the block-tridiagonal line solver of implicit.rs (4x4 blocks that are diagonal: entries 0 and 5 hold the k and omega coefficients, entries 10 and 15 are 1 on the diagonal block and 0 elsewhere).

A1
Model constants of the SST model (see the module documentation); public so that GPU ports can take them from here.
AUPOIX_ITERATIONS
Fixed-point iterations of aupoix_wall (and its GPU twin). The map u_tau^2 -> (nu + nu_t,w) U / d contracts by at most about 0.65 per iteration, from a start that is up to a factor 1 + nu_t,w / nu (10 and more at a leading edge) below the fixed point: 4 iterations leave that error unresolved and the plate of G2r did not converge (density residual stuck at 0.5-1), 5 or more converge; 16 leave under 1e-3.

NU_T_WALL_MAX
Upper bound of nu_t,w / nu: the cap 1e5 / re_c of the eddy viscosity of the cells (eddy_viscosity) in units of the free-stream nu. Where the smooth omega_w bound is active (d large against the roughness) nu_t,w grows with u_tau^2 and the fixed point has no solution without it.
ROUGH_RAMP_CELLS
Number of wall cells over which the roughness is phased in from the wall start (Viscous::wall_i_start): the roughness of the first wall cells is ks rough_ramp(i). A roughness wall state that starts as a step feeds turbulence (k_w with the low rough omega_w) into the slip cells ahead of the wall start, where it spreads up their column (mu_t / mu 15-45 against 3 smooth) and the column oscillates with period 2 at transonic speed (M 0.9-1.3, ks / d 6e-4 to 1.2e-3: the stall at 80 000 iterations); a ramp of 2 and 3 cells still oscillates, 4 and 16 do not.

aupoix_wall
Aupoix (2015) “Colebrook” rough-wall state of a no-slip wall cell with kinematic viscosity nu, speed speed, wall distance d and equivalent sand-grain roughness ks > 0: [k_w, omega_w, nu_t,w] with nu_t,w = k_w / omega_w.
omega_inf
Free-stream specific dissipation rate 1e-6 re_c.
rough_ramp
Fraction of the roughness ks that wall cell i carries: min(1, (i + 1 - wall_i_start) / ROUGH_RAMP_CELLS).
wall_distances
Distance of every cell centroid to the nearest no-slip wall face segment of the viscous run v with boundary conditions bc and body block blk and nozzle exit nozzle (jet faces are not walls; f64::INFINITY without a no-slip face and in solid cells): what Solver::set_viscous computes for the turbulence model, for ports that keep their own copy of the field.
