bowshock_cfd

Source

Line-implicit pseudo-time stepping (Beam-Warming-type approximate factorization, “ADI”) for the steady Euler iteration.

One iteration solves the backward-Euler pseudo-time step (I/dt - dR/dU) dU = R with the right-hand side R of the full (second-order, limited) residual Solver::eval_residual and the first-order flux Jacobians on the left, factorised by direction:

  (I - dt J_i) X = dt R           block-tridiagonal system along every i line (one per row j)
  (I - dt J_j) dU = X             block-tridiagonal system along every j line (one per column i)
  U <- U + dU                     (damped by halving until density and pressure are positive)

J_d is the Jacobian of the residual contribution of the fluxes of direction d, with the Rusanov-type linearisation of the face flux F = (F_L + F_R)/2 - lambda (U_R - U_L)/2, lambda = max(|u_n| + c) of the two adjacent states:

  dF/dU_L = (A_L + lambda I)/2,      dF/dU_R = (A_R - lambda I)/2,       A = d(F.n)/dU  (`flux_jacobian`)

Wall, base-wall and far-field ghost cells enter the first/last row of a line through the Jacobian T = dU_ghost/dU_interior of the ghost state (exact for slip walls, extrapolation and fixed free stream, finite-difference for the characteristic and pressure-outflow BCs; ImplicitParams::implicit_bc = false freezes the ghost states instead).

Every line is independent (lines are solved in parallel); the Jacobian blocks are assembled per cell from the cell states and the face metrics, i.e. the whole step is built from per-face, per-cell and per-line kernels. With ImplicitParams::lines = false the off-diagonal blocks are dropped (point/block-Jacobi implicit scheme, the baseline the line solves are compared against).

The pseudo-time step dt = CFL A / (L_i + L_j) (DtMode::Sum) or CFL A / (2 min(L_i, L_j)) (DtMode::MinDirection, sized by the weaker direction; the factorised solves are unconditionally stable in the stiff direction) with a CFL that grows geometrically from cfl0 to cfl_max over the iterations since Solver::restart_ramp.

Viscous runs (Solver::set_viscous): the no-slip wall faces use the wall ghost (rho, -u, -v, p) (Jacobian diag(1, -1, -1, 1)), the diffusive spectral radius 2 lam I, lam = gamma (mu_f / PRANDTL + mu_t,f / PRANDTL_T) / (rho_f |d|), is added to the dissipation matrix of every face, and the pseudo-time step includes the diffusive limit L_d + 2 lv len_d^2 / A.
