Characteristic (Riemann-invariant) far-field and static-pressure outflow ghost states.

All functions take the primitive state of the first interior cell and the outward unit normal (nx, nr) of the boundary face and return the ghost (boundary) state; both ghost layers are filled with it. They are pure functions of their inputs so they port 1:1 to WGSL.

§Characteristic far field (characteristic_ghost)

With the outward normal n, u_n = v.n, c the sound speed and g = gamma, the one-dimensional (normal to the boundary) Riemann invariants are

  R_out = u_n,i + 2 c_i/(g-1)     carried out of the domain by the u_n + c wave  (from the interior state)
  R_in  = u_n,e - 2 c_e/(g-1)     carried into the domain by the u_n - c wave    (from the free stream)
  u_n,b = (R_out + R_in)/2,   c_b = (g-1)/4 (R_out - R_in)

The entropy s = p/rho^g and the tangential velocity are convected with the flow: taken from the interior for outflow (u_n,b >= 0) and from the free stream for inflow. Then rho_b = (c_b^2/(g s))^(1/(g-1)), p_b = rho_b c_b^2/g, v_b = v_ref + (u_n,b - u_n,ref) n.

Which branch is used is decided per face by the interior normal Mach number M_n = u_n,i/c_i (Regime):

M_nregimeghost
<= -1supersonic inflowfree stream
(-1, 0)subsonic inflowRiemann (inflow/outflow split of entropy + tangential velocity by the sign of u_n,b)
[0, 1)subsonic outflowRiemann (same)
>= 1supersonic outflowinterior (zero gradient)

If the interior state is the free stream, every branch returns the free stream (to round-off), so a uniform flow produces no spurious waves.
