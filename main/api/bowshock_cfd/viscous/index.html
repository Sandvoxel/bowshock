Viscous terms of the axisymmetric compressible Navier-Stokes equations (laminar; the eddy-viscosity arrays are carried for the turbulence model but stay zero until it is attached).

Units: rho_inf = 1, c_inf = 1, p_inf = 1/gamma, T = gamma p / rho (T_inf = 1), lengths in nose diameters.

  mu(T)  = (1 / re_c) T^1.5 (1 + S) / (T + S)            S = sutherland = 110.4 K / T_inf[K],  re_c = rho_inf c_inf d / mu_inf
  kappa  = (mu / PRANDTL + mu_t / PRANDTL_T) / (gamma - 1)         q = -kappa grad T        (c_p = 1 / (gamma - 1))

The residual of a cell uses the total face flux F - Fv in place of the Euler flux F and the radial-momentum source (p - t_tt) A / V in place of p A / V (t_tt = hoop stress):

  V dU/dt = - sum_faces w_f (F - Fv).n + (0, 0, (p - t_tt) A, 0),            w_f = len_f r_m
  Fv.n    = [0, txx nx + txr nr, txr nx + trr nr, (u txx + v txr + kappa T_x) nx + (u txr + v trr + kappa T_r) nr]
  div     = u_x + v_r + v / r;   txx = mue (2 u_x - 2/3 div);  trr = mue (2 v_r - 2/3 div);  txr = mue (u_r + v_x);  mue = mu + mu_t
  t_tt    = (mu_c + mu_t,c) (2 v_c / r_c - 2/3 div_c)

Three data-parallel passes, called from Solver::eval_residual after the inviscid face fluxes:

  1. cell_pass: mu(T) and the Green-Gauss gradient (u_x, u_r, v_x, v_r, T_x, T_r) of every fluid cell in the meridian plane, g = (1/A) sum_faces phi_f n_f len_f with phi_f = (phi_c + phi_neighbour) / 2;
  2. face_pass: the face gradient g_f = gbar + ((phi_R - phi_L) / |d| - gbar.e) e (d = distance vector of the two adjacent cell centres, e = d / |d|, gbar the mean of the two cell gradients: a compact normal derivative with the averaged tangential part) and the viscous flux of every face into fvi, fvj; faces on the axis (r_m = 0) and inside the solid block carry no flux;
  3. the cell residual in solver.rs (with hoop_stress and the diffusive time-step limit diffusion_rate).

A state across a boundary is the ghost cell of Solver::w; the solid cell across a wall of a body block is its ghost as well (block ghost in the j direction, wall_ghost across the base wall). A ghost sits at the mirror image of the centre of its cell in the face centre and carries the gradient, viscosity and eddy viscosity of that cell (the eddy viscosity with the sign flipped across a no-slip face, so it vanishes at the wall).

No-slip walls (Walls): the face where the viscous flow starts (Viscous::wall_i_start) and the body surface, base wall and sting surface of a block. The wall ghost of a no-slip face is (rho, -u, -v, p) (adiabatic wall, zero normal pressure gradient), that of a slip face the reflection about the face normal.
