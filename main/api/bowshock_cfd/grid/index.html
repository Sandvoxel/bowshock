Module grid

Expand description

Structured body-fitted grid in the (x, r) meridian plane and its finite-volume metrics.

Node (i, j), 0<=i<=ni, 0<=j<=nj, stored at j*(ni+1)+i. Cell (i, j) is the quad (i,j) (i+1,j) (i+1,j+1) (i,j+1) (counter-clockwise in the (x, r) plane), stored at j*ni+i.

Axisymmetric weighting. Integrating d(r U)/dt + d(r F)/dx + d(r G)/dr = S over a planar cell of area A gives

  V dU/dt = - sum_faces  (r_mid * len) * (F.n)  +  (0, 0, p*A, 0),      V = integral r dA

where every face carries the weight w = len * r_mid (r_mid = mean radius of its end nodes, exact for a straight edge), and V is the exact integral of r over the polygon. The pressure source p*A is the exact counterpart of sum r_mid * p * n_r * len = p*A (Green’s theorem on the polygon), so a uniform pressure field is preserved to round-off. Faces lying on the axis have r_mid = 0 and therefore contribute no flux (no singularity).

cone_grid
Algebraic body-fitted grid. Grid lines of constant i are the vertical lines x = x_i; the lower boundary is the axis r=0 for x<0 and the cone surface for x>=0; the upper boundary is the straight line from (x_up, h_start) to (length, h_end). A grid node sits exactly at the tip x=0.
geometric_offsets

plate_grid
Flat plate as the inner wall of an annulus: x nodes -geometric_offsets(dx_le, x_up, n_up) (reversed) then geometric_offsets(dx_le, length, n_plate); r nodes r0 + geometric_offsets(dr_wall, height, nj) in every column. The leading edge is the node line i = n_up.
