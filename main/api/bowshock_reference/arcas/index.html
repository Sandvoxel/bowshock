Half-scale Arcas sounding-rocket model of NASA TN D-4013 (J. C. Ferris, 1967, Mach 0.60-1.20, https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19670020050) and TN D-4014 (C. D. Babb & D. E. Fuller, 1967, Mach 1.50-4.63, https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19670020031), configuration 1 (“short”, l/d = 18.20).

Geometry is taken from D-4014 Fig. 1. D-4013 Fig. 1 differs by more than 2 % in fin span (2.2 %) and chords (2.8 %, 3.3 %); both values are given in the constants’ docs. All data are figure readings, with the reading uncertainty stated on each table.

Constants§

BASE_DIAMETER
Base diameter, 1.470 in (D-4014 Fig. 1(a) detail A; 0.65 d in D-4013 Fig. 1).
BOATTAIL_LENGTH
Boattail length, 1.810 in (D-4014 Fig. 1(a)); half-angle about 12.2 degrees.
BODY_DIAMETER
Body diameter, 2.250 in (D-4014 Fig. 1(a)).
FIN_ROOT_CHORD
Fin root chord, 3.380 in (D-4014 Fig. 1(b)); D-4013 Fig. 1: 3.285 in.
FIN_SPAN
Fin span from the body surface, 2.103 in (D-4014 Fig. 1(b)); D-4013 Fig. 1: 2.149 in.
FIN_SWEEP
Axial offset of the tip leading edge from the root leading edge, 1.215 in (D-4014 Fig. 1(b), 30 degree leading-edge sweep).
FIN_THICKNESS
Mean fin thickness, 0.123 in: the mean of 0.150 in at the root and 0.096 in at the tip (D-4014 Fig. 1(b); which end is which is a probable reading, not certain).
FIN_TIP_CHORD
Fin tip chord, 2.165 in (D-4014 Fig. 1(b)); D-4013 Fig. 1: 2.093 in.
NOSE_LENGTH
Nose length, 10.600 in (D-4014 Fig. 1(a)). The real nose has tabulated ordinates and reaches full radius at 9.375 in; it is modelled as a tangent ogive of this length (up to 7 % thicker in radius).
SUPERSONIC
(M, C_A - C_A,c fins off, fins on) from D-4014 Fig. 5(a)/(b) (C_A) and Fig. 4(a) (chamber C_A,c), alpha = 0, fin deflection 0. Reading uncertainty +-0.012; the sign of the chamber correction is not stated in the report (inferred: forebody = C_A - C_A,c).
SUPERSONIC_RE_PER_M
Reynolds number per metre of D-4014 (transition strips, turbulent flow).
SUPERSONIC_T0_K
Stagnation temperature of D-4014 at Mach 1.50 to 2.96 [K].
TOTAL_LENGTH
Total length of configuration 1, 40.950 in (D-4014 Fig. 1(a); 18.20 d).
TRANSONIC
(M, C_A,corr fins off, C_A,corr fins on with fin deflection 0) from D-4013 Fig. 11 (short model, alpha = 0): the axial force with the base-annulus pressure force removed, referenced to the body cross-section; it includes boattail, fins and fin anchors. Reading uncertainty +-0.005 on the flat parts, +-0.02 at Mach 0.90 to 1.00.
TRANSONIC_RE_PER_M
Reynolds number per metre of D-4013 (Fig. 1 text; carborundum transition strips, turbulent flow).
TRANSONIC_T0_K
Stagnation temperature of D-4013 [K].
TUBE_LENGTH
Cylinder length, 28.540 in: from the end of the nose to the start of the boattail at station 39.140 in (D-4014 Fig. 1(a)).
