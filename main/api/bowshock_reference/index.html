bowshock-reference: independent reference solutions the solver is validated against (Taylor-Maccoll cone flow, tangent-cone / shock-expansion theory, axisymmetric potential flow) and the published wind-tunnel data used for total-drag validation (arcas: NASA TN D-4013/D-4014 sounding-rocket axial force; tp2005: NASA TP-2005 base pressure with cold air jets).

Modules§

anf
Army-Navy Basic Finner (ANF) reference projectile: geometry and measured static stability derivatives, used to gate the 3D Euler stability solver and to judge the Barrowman-style (Extended) estimate.
arcas
Half-scale Arcas sounding-rocket model of NASA TN D-4013 (J. C. Ferris, 1967, Mach 0.60-1.20, https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19670020050) and TN D-4014 (C. D. Babb & D. E. Fuller, 1967, Mach 1.50-4.63, https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19670020031), configuration 1 (“short”, l/d = 18.20).
base_drag
Empirical base drag of a blunt base in free flight.
potential
Incompressible potential flow past a closed body of revolution at zero incidence: an independent reference for the low-Mach Euler results (no CFD code involved).
shock_expansion
Independent surface-pressure theory for slender bodies of revolution (no CFD involved):
taylor_maccoll
Taylor-Maccoll solution for supersonic flow over a sharp circular cone at zero incidence.
tp2005
NASA TP-2005 (Covell, 1982): afterbody pressures with axisymmetric cold air jets at Mach 1.65 to 2.50 on a strut-mounted ogive-cylinder, https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19820014341/downloads/19820014341.pdf.
