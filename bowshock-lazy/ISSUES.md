# bowshock-lazy: issue list

Problems met while porting the solver to Luma array ops. `Luma` = needs a Luma change (reported in
`~/Documents/luma/Temp.md`), `lazy` = this crate, `core` = the CPU reference.

| # | Area | Status | Issue |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | NVIDIA driver | driver bug (Luma maintainer, 2026-11-13) | Process exit hangs (100% CPU in `libnvidia-gpucomp`) or corrupts the heap (`free(): chunks in smallbin corrupted`) while the driver's shader compiler thread is still running at `exit()`; intermittent (1 in 3 `bench --quick --staging Fluxes` runs), driver 615.71.09. Not a Luma fix; can still hit `bowshock::Analysis` users on this driver. |
| 2 | Luma | fixed (luma main, solid-assigns merge) | Region assigns into a lazy expression (solid block, base grid 156x76) made an SSP-RK3 step far larger than on the block-free grid: f32 `Fusion` 89 launches / 324 µs (vs 12 / 44), Df32 `Fusion` 95 / 658 µs (vs 21 / 144). Causes: shared expressions weighed once per path (40-node ghost chain weighed 10^5+ ops, so tiny ghost pieces got kernels of their own), and cap-forced cuts inside another cut evaluated every live array built on top. Now (`luma_issues` case 3): f32 `Fusion` 14 / 61 µs, Df32 `Fusion` 21 / 183 µs, `Fluxes` 16 / 60-67 and 17 / 174 µs (no block: 14 / 50, 16 / 136). Remaining: cap cuts at the block-ghost and zero-residual assigns (raising the cap made the block-free grid slower). |
| 3 | lazy | fixed | `zero_region` uploaded a fresh `zeros_on` array per call; now a scalar `assign(c(0.0))` (same launches / cuts). |
| 4 | Luma | reported 2026-11-13 | No `stack`: building the `[16, ni, nj]` block arrays for `solve_block_tridiagonal` with `concat::<f32, 16>(0, ..)` of `[1, ni, nj]` parts costs 11.7 µs per stack at 144x70 (expected ~2 µs, a bandwidth-bound copy); six per implicit step = 70 µs, 29% of the step, as much as the four line solves. Region-assigning the 16 parts into a zeros base was ~20x slower. A `stack` op or block solves taking `[Array; 16]` / `[Array; 4]` would remove it. |
| 5 | Luma | reported 2026-11-13 | `reshape` (`array.rs:708`) evaluates its operand: `solve_result.slice(0, k..k+1).reshape([ni, nj])` materialises. Worked around with `take` and precomputed index arrays (stays lazy). |
| 6 | Luma | reported 2026-11-13 | No `is_finite`: the positivity damping uses `abs().lt(1e30)`, relying on NaN comparing false in the generated WGSL. |
| 7 | lazy | open | Implicit step 2.1-2.5x slower than the hand-written kernels (µs/iter, replay): sting 144x70 f32 227 vs 106, Df32 267 vs 144 (Extended); 288x140 541 vs 261, 591 vs 286; base 156x76 240 vs 97, 304 vs 143; 312x152 598 vs 274, 712 vs 303. CPU 2400-11000. Per level-0 step: line solves 82 µs (Luma's cyclic reduction; Thomas 2.5x slower; the per-line starts of the block grid cost nothing), `concat` stacks 70 µs (#4), the rest assembly. |
| 8 | Luma | open (noted 2026-11-13) | `Array<Df32>` has no `tanh`, `exp`, `ln`, `powf`: the SST blending functions `tanh(a^4)`, `tanh(b^2)` are evaluated in f32 in `df32s` (`to_f32().tanh().to_df32()`, `turbulence.rs::tanh`), and `T^1.5` of Sutherland's law is `T sqrt(T)`. Effect: `F1`, `F2` carry an f32 rounding error [INFERENCE: ~6e-8 relative]; the plate's `k`, `omega`, `mu_t` agree with the f64 CPU to 5e-6 after 50 implicit iterations (`tests/viscous.rs`), so nothing needed more. Expected for a full `Df32` model: `tanh` (and `powf`) on `Df32` arrays. |
| 9 | Luma | open (usability) | A region assign through `slice_mut` fails ("cannot take a mutable view of a read-only view") when the base array is itself a read-only `slice` of a lazy expression, e.g. `let mut l = padded.slice(0, 0..n + 1); l.slice_mut(..)`. Only a fresh expression (`pad`, `copy`, ...) is writable, so `viscous.rs::cell_sides` builds the lower / upper side arrays of a face as two `pad`s (`pad([(1, 0)])`, `pad([(0, 1)])`) instead of two slices of one `pad([(1, 1)])`, and `face_sides` uses `Rec::select` with the base-wall mask instead of an assign. Expected: either works (the slice of a pending expression is a pending expression), or the error names the fix. |
| 10 | lazy | open | Viscous implicit step (G3 body, `bench_viscous`, SST, replay, level 0 156x122 / level 1 312x122): lazy f32 480 / 764 us, df32 612 / 942 us, against 263 / 377 (custom `F32`) and 319 / 428 us (custom `Extended`): 1.8-2.2x, as the inviscid scheme (#7). One step is 65 launches / 522 us (f32, level 0): the six `concat` stacks (issue 11 of the README; [INFERENCE: from the kernel label, as in the inviscid profile]) 105 us, the six block line solves 132 us, the four scalar turbulence solves 24 us, the rest ~260 us over ~49 kernels; one fusion cut (`1537 inlined operations > 1536`, `implicit.rs::E::add`). The viscous terms and SST add 38 launches to the implicit step of the inviscid base case (27). |
